How to read the numbers

Some handwritten season totals do not match the available individual entries. For example, the journal reports 14 deer in 2010, while only 12 named animals are currently indexed. Neither figure has been silently changed to make them agree.

After an earlier hunting logbook was lost in a cabin fire, the journal began again in 2000. The 2001 entries mark the first deer season in the new cabin.

Camp kitchen

Buttermilk soup & sage mush

Grocery runs to Rice Lake, big batches of buttermilk soup, sage mush and meals with Steve and Bucky appear year after year. In 2010 the shopping notes included four gallons of soup, 15 pounds of potatoes and three pounds of bacon.

2014

2020

Deer camp during COVID

The smaller camp and Jeff's stay in an Ice Castle are recorded alongside the usual hunts and deer drives. The writer gave the season an unmistakably candid heading.

2018

Six bears at 360

On an opening weekend with few deer, the journal noted that Danny saw six bears. The new gravel driveway also drew admiration that season.

2025

Opening-weekend bucks

On a 50°F, snow-free opening day, Joel recorded a 10-point east of Arnie's 80. Earl followed with a 9-point at Marlin's on Sunday.

About the archive

A growing family record of the hunters, the land, the weather and the stories at Klatt Kamp.

Our approach

Preserve the writer's voice, nicknames and humor. Date entries and harvests are summarized where full transcriptions aren't available. Unknown handwriting is flagged instead of guessed. Missed, wounded and sighted deer are not counted as recovered harvests.

Future seasons

The site is a public, read-only GitHub Pages journal. Family members can browse it without an account. The repository owner can update the records as each new hunting season is added.

Records to verify

Source: family journal photographs and transcriptions, the previous GitHub website, and the working 2010–2025 harvest spreadsheet. No official hunting statistics are claimed.
