About these chronicles

This archive preserves Klatt Kamp's family hunting history, including the hunters, camp traditions, weather, deer drives, harvests, and memorable moments.

Editorial principles

Preserve the writer's original voice, colorful language, nicknames, and humor. Correct minor typographical errors only where the meaning is certain. Mark illegible details for review rather than inventing facts.

Do not treat a sighting, miss, or unrecovered wounded animal as a confirmed harvest. Nubbin bucks are bucks. Counts are provisional until checked against the original photographs.

Adding new seasons

This is a standalone, offline website preview. It does not yet accept edits or publish changes to the internet. The next step is to verify the source material, then select a hosting and contributor workflow.
